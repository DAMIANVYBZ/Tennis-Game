YOUR GAME PLAN

You play. We help you line up.

All input options

PS5: Left stick / D-pad moves in both directions. Hold the right stick in any direction to move the aiming target. Up moves it deeper; down brings it shorter. Release the stick to keep its position. Hold ✕ topspin, □ flat, ○ slice, △ lob; release near contact. R2 sprints. Options pauses.

Keyboard: Arrows / WASD move. Q/E aim left/right. Z/R set shorter/deeper shots. Hold Space topspin, X flat, C slice, V lob. Shift sprints; P pauses.

Touch: Left joystick moves. Hold a shot button; drag left/right while holding to aim, then release. Drag up/down for depth.

Serve into the highlighted diagonal service box. A first fault gives you a second serve; a double fault loses the point. Balls touching the lines count as in. Let serves bounce before returning. Reach the ball before its second bounce. The curved aiming guide shows your planned flight and landing while preparing. Use your selected aiming controls to move it. You can aim beyond the lines; orange warns of an unsafe target; advanced-mode timing errors can still change the actual shot. The incoming landing ring helps you read the bounce. Aim for open space. Assisted contact swings automatically when you move into reach. You control positioning, aim, power and shot type. Advanced mode requires manual swing timing.
